## v0.8.3 implications

Potential joint questions include:
- whether local regulation coordinates preserve useful information about whole-system adaptation;
- how coupling, information exchange, diversity, disagreement and environment condition realized local behavior;
- when aggregation destroys useful diversity or hides failure;
- when no coherent scalar exists.
